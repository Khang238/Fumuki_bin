# Fumuki — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 26, 2026

### 1. Information We May Collect
Depending on how you use Fumuki, we may collect information such as messages, account identifiers, usage logs, and information required to operate the service.

### 2. How We Use Information
Information may be used to:
- Provide and maintain Fumuki's features.
- Generate AI responses and process tool requests.
- Improve service performance, reliability, and security.
- Prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical issues.

### 3. AI Processing and Third-Party Services
Your messages or relevant information may be sent to AI providers or third-party tools to process your requests. These services may handle data under their own privacy policies and terms.

### 4. Data Storage and Security
We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

### 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate Fumuki, or when required by law.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable requirements. To request deletion of your information, contact the Fumuki team.

### 7. Children's Privacy
Fumuki is not intended for use in violation of applicable age requirements. Users should follow the age restrictions that apply to the service and their location.

### 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published with an updated revision date.

### 9. Contact
For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact the Fumuki team through our official support channel.
